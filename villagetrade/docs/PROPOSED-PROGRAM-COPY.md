# Proposed program copy — FOR FOUNDER REVIEW, NOT PUBLISHED

**None of this is in the application.** `lib/programs.ts` holds empty strings

## Principles I wrote to

- No claim the service is operating, available, or has ever been used
- No testimonial, no count, no saving, no outcome
- No promise VillageTrade cannot keep — no vetting claim, no safety guarantee
- Plain language; these are practical services for people under pressure
- Neutral on gender for both signature programs, per your ruling

---

## A Good Man for the Day™

> Some jobs need a second pair of hands for an afternoon — a door rehung, a
> room cleared, a load taken to the dump, a list of small repairs that has
> been waiting since spring.
>
> Book a neighbour for a single task, a half day, or a full day. Pay, trade,
> or do some of each.
>
> Open to qualified providers regardless of gender.

## A Good Woman for the Day™

> Some jobs need a second pair of hands for an afternoon — a house readied
> for visitors, a kitchen reorganised, errands run, a list of small things
> that never reach the top of the pile.
>
> Book a neighbour for a single task, a half day, or a full day. Pay, trade,
> or do some of each.
>
> Open to qualified providers regardless of gender.

**Note on these two.** They are deliberately parallel and the work described
is interchangeable. If the names are to mean "a person who shows up and
helps" rather than a division of labour by gender, the descriptions should
not quietly reinstate one. I have kept them close for that reason — but if
you intend a real distinction, tell me what it is and I will write to it.

## VillageTrade Holiday Helping Hands™

> The holidays bring a longer list than usual: lights to hang, a house to
> ready, cooking for more people than normal, driving, lifting, clearing up
> afterwards.
>
> Ask a neighbour for help with one task or a day of them, across
> Thanksgiving, Hanukkah, Christmas, Kwanzaa, New Year's and other
> celebrations. Pay, trade, or do some of each.

## Holiday Help Wishlist™

> Write down everything you need help with, and let neighbours offer.
>
> Add as many tasks as you like. A provider can offer on one, or propose a
> bundle covering several — often easier for both of you than arranging each
> separately.
