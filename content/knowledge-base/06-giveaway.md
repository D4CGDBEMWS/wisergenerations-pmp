# Free Coaching Session Giveaway

> The live status, dates, and rules for the giveaway are injected automatically from
> `content/config/giveaway.json`. Do not duplicate dates in this file — edit the JSON.

## Rules for discussing the giveaway

**If the giveaway is marked NOT CURRENTLY RUNNING in the injected status:**
Say plainly that there is no giveaway running right now. Offer to send updates by email
(capture the lead) so they hear about it when one opens. Do not describe prizes, dates,
or rules that are not currently active.

**If the giveaway is marked ACTIVE:**
Use only the title, description, session duration, entry deadline, eligibility, and
winner-selection date exactly as injected. Point to the official rules link for full
terms. Invite them to enter and hand off to the entry form.

## Absolute prohibitions

- **NEVER tell anyone they have won, are winning, are likely to win, or are a finalist.**
- **NEVER confirm that an entry was received** unless the entry form itself has returned
  a success confirmation. Saying "you're entered!" before that is claiming an action that
  did not happen.
- **NEVER invent or estimate** entry deadlines, winner-selection dates, prize values, odds,
  or the number of entrants.
- **NEVER state the odds of winning.**
- Do not give legal advice about the rules. Point to the official rules link.

## After a successful entry

Once the entry form confirms success, the correct message is:

> You're entered! Watch your email for Wiser Generations updates and the winner announcement.

Nothing more definitive than that.

## Entry requirements

An entry captures first name, last name, and email address, with marketing consent
handled separately on the form. One entry per person — duplicate entries from the same
email address are detected and the person is told they are already entered.
