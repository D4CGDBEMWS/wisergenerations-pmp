# PMI Exam Eligibility

## PMP® eligibility requirements (set by PMI, not by Wiser Generations)

A candidate needs ONE of the following, plus 35 contact hours of formal PM education:

- A four-year degree **plus** 36 months of project leadership experience in the last 8 years, **OR**
- A high school diploma / GED **plus** 60 months of project leadership experience in the last 8 years

The Wiser Generations PMP program provides 36 hours of PMI-aligned education, which
satisfies the 35 contact-hour requirement.

## CAPM® eligibility requirements

Lower bar than the PMP. A candidate needs:
- A secondary degree (high school diploma, associate degree, or global equivalent)
- 23 hours of project management education — which the CAPM program fulfills

**No prior project management work experience is required for the CAPM.**

## "I've never had the title Project Manager — do I qualify?"

Yes, potentially. PMI evaluates the experience described, not the job title. Leading
projects, managing stakeholders, coordinating resources, and being accountable for
deliverables all count, even under a different title. Crystal helps students document
their experience in a way that is clear, accurate, and PMI-compliant.

## Military experience and PMP eligibility

Military leadership experience — managing logistics, coordinating teams, overseeing
operations, running missions — maps directly to PMI's requirements. The Veterans PM
Pathway includes a military-to-PM skills translation guide.

## PMI application process

The application itself typically takes a few hours once experience documentation is
gathered. PMI usually reviews applications within 5–10 business days. If selected for
audit (a random process), additional documentation is required, which can add 2–4 weeks.
Crystal walks all students through the application inside the program.

## PMP vs CAPM — which one?

- **PMP®** is for experienced project managers (3–5+ years of leadership experience required).
- **CAPM®** is the entry-level credential — no PM experience required.
- New to PM → start with CAPM®. Have the experience → go straight to PMP®.

## Eligibility judgment calls

Do **not** issue a definitive ruling on whether a specific person qualifies. Explain the
published requirements, then recommend a free strategy call where Crystal reviews their
actual background and walks them through the application. Eligibility depends on details
this assistant cannot verify.
