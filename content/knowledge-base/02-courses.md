# Courses and Programs

> Pricing is NOT stored in this file. Live prices are injected automatically from

> price, edit `lib/constants.ts`.

## PMP® Certification Prep — the flagship program

For working professionals ready to earn the gold standard in project management.

**Format:** Mentor-led, cohort-based, live virtual sessions.
**Length:** Approximately 8–12 weeks depending on the cohort schedule.
**Study commitment:** Most students spend 8–12 hours per week between live sessions,
reading, and practice exams.

**Included at every tier:**
- 36 hours of PMI-aligned education (satisfies PMI's 35 contact-hour requirement)
- Live virtual cohort sessions
- Practice bank: 694 questions plus a full-length mock exam
- Study guide

Higher tiers add 1:1 mentorship, exam application support, a pass guarantee,
personalized study plans, career coaching, and LinkedIn optimization.
There are three tiers: Essentials, Professional (most popular), and Executive.

## CAPM® Career Launcher

For early-career professionals and career changers entering project management.
No prior PM work experience is required to qualify for the CAPM® exam.

**Length:** Typically 6–8 weeks.

**Included:**
- 23 hours of PMI-aligned education
- Cohort access
- Practice exam bank
- Study guide

The Professional tier adds a career transition roadmap, resume and LinkedIn makeover,
exam application support, and PM job search strategy.

## Veterans PM Pathway

For military veterans, active duty service members, and military spouses transitioning
to civilian careers.

- Military-to-PM skills translation guide
- Choice of PMP® or CAPM® prep
- Veteran peer cohort — train alongside fellow veterans and service members
- Employer introduction program
- Veteran discount applied

Proof of service is requested at enrollment.

**GI Bill / VA benefits:** Wiser Generations Int'l is *working toward* VA approval for
applicable benefit programs. It is NOT currently approved. Anyone asking about GI Bill,
VA benefits, or VET TEC should be told the current status honestly and offered a call
with Crystal to explore all available funding options, including employer reimbursement.

## Corporate PM Training

Custom PM training for teams and organizations, delivered virtually or on-site in
Metro Atlanta.

- Customized curriculum tailored to the client's industry and projects
- Team cohort format
- PMI education-hours documentation
- Executive briefings available
- Volume pricing for 5+ employees
- **Pricing is custom** — quoted after a consultation. Never quote a corporate price.

## PMP® 1-on-1 Coaching

For professionals who want personal support.

- Bi-weekly coaching sessions
- Custom study plan
- Direct mentor access
- Flexible 4-week or 8-week packages
- **Pricing is custom** — discussed on a call.

## Delivery format

All individual programs are delivered virtually via live online sessions, accessible
nationwide. In-person and hybrid options are available for corporate teams in Metro
Atlanta.

**If a student misses a session:** Session recordings are typically available. Crystal
also holds regular office hours and Q&A sessions between cohort meetings. Missing an
occasional session will not derail a student.
