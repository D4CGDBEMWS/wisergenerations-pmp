# Cohort Dates, Enrollment, and Payment

## Cohort dates — READ THIS CAREFULLY

**There are no specific published cohort start dates in this knowledge base.**

Cohorts run on a rolling basis and the website shows the next open cohorts by month.
Exact start dates, session times, and current seat availability change regularly and are
confirmed directly with the team.

**Correct response when asked "when does the next cohort start?":**
Explain that cohort dates are updated regularly, that the fastest way to get current
availability is a free strategy call or the Programs page, and offer the scheduling link.
Mention that cohorts have limited spots to maintain quality, so early enrollment is
recommended.

**NEVER state, guess, estimate, or imply a specific cohort start date, month, or
enrollment deadline.** If the owner adds dates to this file later, use only what is
written here verbatim.

## Enrollment path

1. Book a free 30-minute strategy call with Crystal (recommended first step for most people)
2. Or enroll directly through the Programs or Enroll page
3. Checkout is handled securely through Stripe

## Payment

- **Payment plans are available.** Options are discussed during the free strategy call.
  Crystal works with students to find a payment structure that fits their situation.
- **What tuition includes:** all live cohort sessions, the practice exam bank, the
  personalized study plan, exam application support, mentor access to Crystal, and
  (on qualifying tiers) the pass guarantee. There are no hidden fees or add-ons.
- **What tuition does NOT include:** the PMI exam registration fee, which is paid
  directly to PMI. For PMI members the PMP exam fee is $405; for non-members it is $555.
  Crystal recommends becoming a PMI member first — the member discount on the exam fee
  more than covers the annual membership cost, and membership includes the PMBOK Guide.
- **Employer reimbursement:** A tuition justification letter and itemized invoice are
  provided for submission to an employer's L&D program.

## Pass guarantee

PMP® Professional and Executive tier students are covered by the pass guarantee: if a
student completes the program and does not pass, they restudy free. A debrief with
Crystal identifies weak areas and builds a focused re-test plan.

The guarantee is for students who commit to the process — full terms are on the
Guarantee page. Direct anyone asking for specific guarantee conditions to that page
rather than paraphrasing the terms.

## Refunds and cancellations

**Refund terms are NOT in this knowledge base.** Direct all refund, cancellation, and
billing questions to the Terms page or to a human via the contact options. Do not
describe, estimate, or imply any refund policy.
