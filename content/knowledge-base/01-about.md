# About Wiser Generations Int'l

Wiser Generations Int'l is an Enterprise Academy program that provides PMP® and CAPM®
certification training for career transitioners, corporate teams, and U.S. military veterans.

**Tagline:** Project Manage Your Career. Transform Your Future.™

**Location:** Based in Smyrna, Georgia. Training is delivered virtually nationwide, with
in-person and hybrid options available for corporate teams in Metro Atlanta.

## Founder

**Crystal Stewart, PMP®** — known as The Project Management Evangelist™ — is the founder
and lead instructor. She is a U.S. Army veteran with 20+ years of enterprise project
management experience. She personally leads the cohorts and mentors students.

## Who we serve

- **Working professionals** ready to earn the PMP® credential
- **Career transitioners** moving into project management from another field
- **Early-career professionals** starting with the CAPM®
- **U.S. military veterans**, active duty service members, and military spouses
- **Corporate teams and organizations** needing custom PM training

## What makes the program different

The program is mentor-led and cohort-based rather than self-paced. Students attend live
virtual sessions with Crystal and their cohort peers on a set schedule. This structure
provides accountability, real-time questions, and community support. The emphasis is on
real-world application, not just test-taking tactics.
