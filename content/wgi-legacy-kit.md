## How to use this kit

1. Read the notices above and sign them.
2. Fill in one section at a time, in order. Use pencil; plans change.
3. Keep the first 90 days simple: **save, invest, and protect** your family spiritually, physically and emotionally through stewardship.
4. **Move in silence until day 90.** Share your plan only with the people filling it in with you. Silence never covers harm (see the safety notice).
5. Meet as a family once a month to update the scorecard, and once a year (each October) to review the whole kit.

## Family constitution starter

A family constitution is a written agreement about how your family lives and decides. It is not a legal document; your attorney turns the money and property parts into a trust.

1. **Our foundation:** We live by \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (for example, the Bible as our guide).
2. **How we decide:** One person, one vote at family meetings; decisions need \_\_\_\_\_\_ of \_\_\_\_\_\_ votes.
3. **Two witnesses:** No family member is judged or removed on one person's word alone. A family judgment needs two or more witnesses or clear evidence (Deuteronomy 19:15).
4. **The one exception:** Anyone who harms a child or an elder is permanently out of the family circle. This is reported to authorities right away; reporting never waits for witnesses, a vote, or day 90.
5. **Freedom to leave:** Any adult may step away from the plan at any time without punishment.
6. **Faith is invited, never forced.**
7. **Joining from outside:** A person joining by marriage or invitation is welcomed after \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (for example, shared faith, commitment to unity, and a real contribution).
8. **Money rules:** No mixing family-company money with personal money; every loan or sponsorship is in writing.
9. **Next leaders:** We train \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to lead after us.
10. **Review:** We review this starter every October.
